# Sylvester Hermann Gottlieb — Research Update
**Research date:** 2026-08-26

## Purpose

This file records the August 26, 2026 update to the ongoing research project on **Sylvester Hermann Gottlieb**, born in Vienna in 1871 and later active in the hotel industry in Zürich and Baden-Baden.

This is an **update to the existing project**, not a clean-sheet biography. Older findings should be retained where they remain correct, while new evidence should be used to strengthen, qualify, or correct earlier conclusions.

## 2. Savoy Hotel Baur en Ville, Zürich

### Directorship

Contemporary hotel advertising explicitly identifies:

> **S. H. Gottlieb, Direktor**

at the **Savoy Hotel Baur en Ville** in Zürich.

This is important because it establishes Gottlieb's role from contemporary commercial material rather than relying only on later hotel histories.

A useful digitized source is:

https://repozytorium.biblos.pk.edu.pl/redo/resources/39044/file/scans/DEFAULT/OCR_rezultaty/100000298978_A_v1_200dpi_q60.pdf

The advertisement describes the Savoy as having approximately 200 beds and refers to the major 1908 rebuilding and refurnishing before naming **S. H. Gottlieb** as director.

### Probable starting date

A modern management chronology places a Gottlieb at the Savoy beginning around **1910**, following Hermann Duffing.

Source:

https://famoushotels.org/hotels/savoy-baur-en-ville

That source reportedly gives the name as **"I. Gottlieb"**, but this conflicts with contemporary evidence explicitly naming **S. H. Gottlieb**. The "I." should therefore be treated as a probable transcription or typographical error unless independent evidence demonstrates otherwise.

### Working conclusion

The beginning of Gottlieb's Savoy directorship can now reasonably be described as approximately **1910**.

**Assessment: VERY STRONG.**

## 5. Grand Hotel Victoria, Zürich

Earlier research produced evidence that Gottlieb had managed the Savoy for a substantial period and apparently moved in **autumn 1923** to the **Grand Hotel Victoria** in Zürich.

The Grand Hotel Victoria was a major hotel at **Bahnhofplatz 9, Zürich**.

The current research pass did not produce a second independent contemporary source clearly naming Gottlieb at the Victoria.

### Working conclusion

The move to the Grand Hotel Victoria remains **strongly supported by the previously found contemporary evidence**, but it should still be treated as needing one additional primary-source confirmation.

High-priority targets:
- Zürich address directories for 1923–1928,
- Zürich Einwohnerkontrolle / registration records,
- Swiss hotel-industry publications,
- contemporary newspaper advertisements and hotel notices.

**Assessment: STRONG, NOT YET FULLY CLOSED.**

## 6. Hotel Messmer, Baden-Baden

### 1929 association

Newly reviewed ephemera strengthens Gottlieb's association with **Hotel Messmer** in Baden-Baden by **1929**.

Surviving period advertisements are catalogued as 1929 Hotel Messmer material explicitly associated with Gottlieb.

Example listing:

https://www.ebay.de/itm/406719717109

A second listing for apparently similar material was also identified:

https://www.ebay.de/itm/327013572828

These marketplace descriptions are not ideal substitutes for examining the original advertisement itself, but they materially strengthen the chronology and justify stating that Gottlieb was associated with Hotel Messmer by 1929.

### Exact role still needs clarification

The precise legal/business status remains unresolved:
- director,
- tenant,
- operator,
- proprietor,
- concessionaire,
- or another managerial role.

Earlier telephone-directory evidence reportedly used wording equivalent to **"Inhaber S. H. Gottlieb"**, which would suggest proprietor/operator, but this must now be interpreted in light of the hotel's closure.

**Assessment for 1929 association: STRONG.**

---

## 7. Important correction: Hotel Messmer appears to have closed in 1930

The Baden-Baden municipal historical project / Gedenkbuch refers to Hotel Messmer as:

> **"schon seit 1930 geschlossenen Hotels Messmer"**

that is, the Hotel Messmer **already closed since 1930**.

Relevant source:

https://gedenkbuch.baden-baden.de/person/seelberg-ernst/

Separate contemporary material also documents the liquidation or sale of hotel furnishings around this period.

This creates an important conflict with earlier research that appeared to identify **1932 and 1934 telephone-directory entries** naming:

> Hotel Messmer, Inhaber S. H. Gottlieb

### Revised interpretation

The later telephone-directory entries can **no longer be used as straightforward proof that Gottlieb was operating an open hotel through 1934**.

Possible explanations include:
1. stale directory information,
2. continued formal association with the property after hotel operations ceased,
3. reuse of the Hotel Messmer business name,
4. an error in the year assigned to a scanned directory,
5. a need to recheck the transcription of the directory entry.

### Correct wording for the website

Do **not** state:

> Gottlieb operated Hotel Messmer until at least 1934.

Use instead:

> Gottlieb was associated with Hotel Messmer by 1929. Historical evidence indicates that the hotel closed in 1930. Later telephone-directory references apparently continued to associate S. H. Gottlieb with Hotel Messmer, but the significance of those entries remains unresolved.

**Assessment: MATERIAL CORRECTION TO EARLIER INTERPRETATION.**

## 8. Later Hotel Messmer ownership and archival trail

By the mid-1930s, the Hotel Messmer property formed part of a property administration associated with **J. Lassmann**, with **Ernst Seelberg** later involved in the administration.

The Baden-Baden Gedenkbuch identifies archival material that may contain evidence concerning the ownership, leases, debts, management, or transfer of the Hotel Messmer property.

One useful archival reference is:

**Staatsarchiv Freiburg, F 196/1 Nr. 3834**

Relevant finding aid / archival context:

https://www2.landesarchiv-bw.de/provenienzforschung/stichwort-4039457-8.html

### Research significance

These files may be substantially more useful than broad web searching because they could contain:
- purchase agreements,
- deeds,
- leases,
- creditor records,
- inventories,
- correspondence,
- previous owner/operator details,
- documentation of the 1929–1930 closure.

If Gottlieb had a formal interest in Hotel Messmer, these records may identify it precisely.

## 9. Revised working chronology

### 1871 — Vienna
**8 December 1871:** Sylvester Hermann Gottlieb born in Vienna.

### 1871 — Vienna
**10 December 1871:** Baptized at St. Johann Nepomuk.

### c. 1910 — Zürich
Probably begins serving as director of the **Savoy Hotel Baur en Ville**.

### 1910s — Zürich
Contemporary advertising explicitly identifies:

> **S. H. Gottlieb, Direktor**

at the Savoy Hotel Baur en Ville.

### 1922 — Zürich
Previously identified evidence continues to place Gottlieb at the Savoy as director.

### Autumn 1923 — Zürich
Apparently leaves the Savoy and moves to the **Grand Hotel Victoria**.

### 1924
Previously located Austrian press evidence describes Gottlieb as Vienna-born and reports his Zürich hotel career.

### 1929 — Baden-Baden
Gottlieb is associated with **Hotel Messmer**.

### 1930 — Baden-Baden
Hotel Messmer appears to close; liquidation/sale activity follows.

### 1932–1934 — Baden-Baden?
Earlier telephone-directory references apparently continue to associate "S. H. Gottlieb" with Hotel Messmer, but these entries now require reinterpretation and cannot be treated as proof that the hotel was still operating.

### 1935 onward
The Hotel Messmer property is associated with the J. Lassmann property administration and Ernst Seelberg.

### Death
**Still unresolved.**

## 10. Evidence grading

### Confirmed / very strong
- Sylvester Hermann Gottlieb was born in Vienna on **8 December 1871**.
- He was baptized at **St. Johann Nepomuk** on **10 December 1871**.
- His parents were **Johann Georg Gottlieb** and **Franziska Leschinger**.
- Contemporary advertising identifies **S. H. Gottlieb as director of the Savoy Hotel Baur en Ville**.
- The identification of the Savoy director S. H. Gottlieb with Sylvester Hermann Gottlieb of Vienna is now **very strong**.

### Strong
- Gottlieb probably began his Savoy directorship around **1910**.
- He apparently moved to the **Grand Hotel Victoria** in autumn **1923**.
- He was associated with **Hotel Messmer by 1929**.

### Corrected / unresolved
- The earlier interpretation that Gottlieb operated Hotel Messmer through at least 1934 is no longer secure.
- Evidence indicates that Hotel Messmer had closed by **1930**.
- 1932/1934 telephone-directory entries require fresh verification and interpretation.
- Gottlieb's movements after the Hotel Messmer period remain unresolved.
- His date and place of death remain unknown.

4. Add the **1938 Schulthess centenary history** as a key research source.

8. Correct the previous implication that Hotel Messmer remained in normal operation through 1934.
9. Explicitly state that the **1932/1934 directory evidence is unresolved** in light of the documented 1930 closure.
