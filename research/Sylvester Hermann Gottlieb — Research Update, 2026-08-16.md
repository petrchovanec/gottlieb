# Summary of this research update

This research pass produced two particularly important additions:

1. A **1914 contemporary travel guide** identifies **S. H. Gottlieb as Direktor of the Savoy-Hotel Baur en Ville in Zürich**. This adds a new independently documented point between earlier evidence around 1911 and the previously known 1922 evidence.

2. A **20 March 1931 Baden source gives the full name "Sylvester Hermann Gottlieb"** and identifies him as a **Hotelier** and **Pächter des Hotels Meßmer** in Baden-Baden. This is currently the strongest evidence for his full name in connection with the hotel profession.

The research also uncovered evidence that Hotel Messmer had already been described as **closed since 1930**, and that the property belonged to the Lassmann property complex. Consequently, Gottlieb should presently be described as **lessee/operator (Pächter)** of Hotel Messmer rather than its proven owner.

The major unresolved problem remains the biographical link to the reported **1871 Vienna birth**. No source located during this research pass establishes Gottlieb's exact birth date, parents, spouse, citizenship, or death.

### Repository

### Underlying source inspected

**Yes.** The digitized contemporary newspaper/trade-paper page and OCR were inspected.

### Evidence status

**Verified primary/contemporary evidence — very strong.**

### Claims supported

This source establishes that:

- a hotelier named **Sylvester Hermann Gottlieb** was in Baden-Baden;
- his occupation was explicitly **Hotelier**;
- he was associated with **Hotel Meßmer / Hotel Messmer**;
- his legal/business status was **Pächter**, i.e. lessee/operator, rather than necessarily owner;
- a bankruptcy proceeding concerning him had reached the `Aufhebungen` stage by 20 March 1931.

### Important limitation

The entry does **not** state why the bankruptcy proceeding was terminated.

Do not infer `mangels Masse` unless another source explicitly says so.

### Significance for identity research

This is particularly important because Zürich hotel sources normally identify the Savoy director only as **S. H. Gottlieb**.

The full Baden-Baden name:

**Sylvester Hermann Gottlieb**

fits those initials exactly and concerns a man in the same profession within a chronologically plausible career sequence.

This strongly supports, but does not by itself conclusively prove, that the Baden-Baden hotelier and the former Zürich hotel director were the same person.

# C. Conflicting evidence

## C1. Modern Savoy management chronology conflicts with contemporary evidence

A modern Famous Hotels history gives approximately:

- 1909–1910 — Hermann Duffing
- 1910–1918 — `I. Gottlieb`
- 1918–1926 — Fritz Giger

### URL

https://famoushotels.org/hotels/savoy-baur-en-ville

### Evidence status

**Modern secondary source — conflicting with contemporary evidence.**

### Conflict

The 1922 Dossenbach guide explicitly identifies:

> S. H. Gottlieb, dir

at Savoy-Baur en Ville.

Therefore, the statement that Gottlieb's tenure definitively ended in **1918** should not currently be used as established fact.

### Possible explanations

Possible explanations include:

- an error in the modern chronology;
- Fritz Giger having a different management role;
- Gottlieb retaining a formal/public directorship;
- the 1922 guide carrying outdated information.

No explanation has yet been proved.

### Website treatment

Prefer wording such as:

> Gottlieb is documented as director by 1914 and was still listed as director in a 1922 Swiss travel guide.

Do not presently write:

> Gottlieb served from 1910 to 1918.

unless clearly attributed to the secondary source and marked as conflicting.

### Website treatment

- Jakob Lassmann, described as Swiss;
- Malwine/Malvine née Zucker.

Jakob Lassmann reportedly died in Luzern in 1929.

### Relevant Baden source

https://gedenkbuch.baden-baden.de/person/seelberg-margarete-geb-lassmann/

### Evidence status

**Interesting hypothesis, not yet established identity evidence.**

### Research question

Determine whether the Jakob Lassmann connected with Baur en Ville in Zürich was the same Jakob Lassmann whose family later owned the Hotel Messmer property in Baden-Baden.

If confirmed, this would establish a noteworthy property/business connection between the two hotels associated with Gottlieb.

However, it would **not automatically prove that Lassmann personally caused or arranged Gottlieb's move from Zürich to Baden-Baden**.
