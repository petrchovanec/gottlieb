## 1. Purpose of this file

This file consolidates research concerning **Sylvester Hermann Gottlieb**, a Vienna-born hotelier associated with Zürich and later apparently with Baden-Baden.

It is intended to serve as the authoritative research layer for a public website.

The website must **not treat every item in this file as an established historical fact**. Each item has an evidence status.

### Evidence-status vocabulary

- **VERIFIED-DIRECT** — the underlying source or sufficiently detailed source record has been inspected.
- **PRIOR-CHAT-VERIFIED** — the source was inspected during earlier research, but the exact scan/page should be reacquired before publication if possible.
- **CATALOG-VERIFIED** — existence and bibliographic identity of the source are verified, but its contents have not yet been inspected.
- **USER-SUPPLIED** — information supplied through family/genealogical research but not independently established here.
- **INDEXED-LEAD** — a catalog, search result, auction listing, OCR index, or similar record indicates a promising source, but the underlying primary source has not been adequately inspected.
- **NEEDS-RECHECK** — an earlier research finding exists, but there is currently insufficient documentation to publish it as fact.
- **CONFLICT** — a source conflicts with stronger or other evidence.

**Rule:** Codex must never silently promote an INDEXED-LEAD, NEEDS-RECHECK, or USER-SUPPLIED item into an established fact.

# 3. Executive summary

The strongest documentary evidence presently concerns an **S. H. Gottlieb who was director of the Savoy-Baur en Ville in Zürich**.

A 1922 Swiss travel guide lists the Savoy-Baur en Ville and identifies **“S. H. Gottlieb, dir.”** The source is Frederick Dossenbach's *How to See Switzerland: A Practical Guide*, copyrighted in 1922. This is currently the strongest directly re-verified public source connecting S. H. Gottlieb with the hotel. citeturn943905view0turn888992view0

Other evidence found during the research also associates **“Direktion S.H. Gottlieb”** with the Baur en Ville, including an extant historic hotel postcard catalogued by an auction house. citeturn943905view2

Earlier research also identified what appeared to be:

- a **1911 advertisement** naming S. H. Gottlieb as director of the Savoy/Baur en Ville;
- a possible **1924 connection with Zürich's Grand Hotel Victoria**;
- a later connection with **Hotel Messmer in Baden-Baden**, including telephone-directory references from the early 1930s.

The 1911 and Baden-Baden findings are strong enough to pursue but their exact original pages should be reacquired before they become formal website citations.

The Grand Hotel Victoria connection is presently less secure and must be classified as **NEEDS-RECHECK**.

Most importantly, public sources using only **S. H. Gottlieb** still need to be linked conclusively to **Sylvester Hermann Gottlieb, born Vienna in 1871** by a person-level record such as a Zürich municipal registration card or Baden-Baden Meldekarte.

# 5. Important source conflict: the Savoy management timeline

A current secondary history of the Savoy Baur en Ville gives the following management sequence:

- 1909–1910 — Hermann Duffing
- 1910–1918 — “I. Gottlieb”
- 1918–1926 — Fritz Giger

The source therefore apparently places a Gottlieb at the hotel from 1910–1918 but identifies him as **“I. Gottlieb”**, not S. H. Gottlieb, and says Fritz Giger became manager in 1918. citeturn547743search15

This conflicts with the **direct 1922 source**, which identifies **S. H. Gottlieb as director**. citeturn943905view0

**Status: CONFLICT**

Possible explanations include:

1. “I. Gottlieb” is a transcription or typographical error for S. H. Gottlieb.
2. The dates in the secondary timeline are incorrect.
3. “Director” and another managerial position were held concurrently.
4. There were two different management functions.
5. The 1922 guide reproduced information that was not fully current.
6. There were multiple Gottliebs.

None of these explanations should be adopted without evidence.

### Research priority

Consult primary Savoy records, Zürich address books, advertisements, municipal registration records, and the 1938 centenary history before resolving the chronology.

Secondary-source URL:

https://famoushotels.org/hotels/savoy-baur-en-ville

### Do not yet infer

Do not infer from a 1934 directory entry alone that Gottlieb:

- was alive throughout 1934;
- personally owned the hotel property;
- died after a specific date;
- lived at the hotel.

Directories can contain publication lag and the meaning of *Inhaber* should be interpreted in the commercial context.

# 27. Rules for future AI/Codex work

These rules should also be copied into `AGENTS.md`.

## Historical accuracy

1. Never invent a fact.
2. Never invent a date.
3. Never invent a citation.
4. Never silently transform an inference into a fact.
5. Preserve uncertainty.
6. Prefer primary sources.
7. If two sources conflict, display the conflict rather than choosing the convenient version.
8. A secondary source does not override contradictory contemporary evidence without explanation.

## Identity

Do not automatically assume every “Hermann Gottlieb,” “S. Gottlieb,” or “Gottlieb” is this person.

Require corroborating details such as:

- occupation
- hotel
- location
- birth
- residence
- chronology

## Source handling

Every source receives a stable ID.

Never reuse IDs.

Never delete a source because its URL dies.

Instead mark:

```text
url_status: unavailable
```

and preserve its bibliographic description.

## New findings

For each new source:

1. Add it to the source catalogue.
2. Assess its evidence status.
3. Identify the claims it supports or contradicts.
4. Add or update claim records.
5. Update timeline events where necessary.
6. Only then update the narrative biography.
7. Record the change in the site's research log.

## Conflicts

A conflict must remain visible until resolved by evidence.

Example:

**SRC-001** says S. H. Gottlieb was director in 1922.

**SRC-018** gives another manager for 1918–1926.

The code or prose must not silently choose one chronology.

## Research leads

Research leads should live in the repository but remain visually distinct from established history.

Suggested labels:

- Verified
- Probable
- Unresolved
- Research Lead
- Conflicting Evidence
